In short

  1. Take the thermal pictures on a cold evening and get the original files off the camera.
  2. On the home page, choose up to {{ batch_size }} pictures and press Process Batch. The site reads the temperatures and marks the places that are warmer than the wall around them.
  3. Open the batch. Say what each marked point is (window, door, wall...) and give it a number. Add the address and any notes.
  4. Press Save & Generate Report, then print the report or save it as a PDF.

A batch is one set of pictures that becomes one report, usually one property.

Taking pictures

  • Cold and dark is best. The bigger the difference between indoors and outdoors, the clearer the heat loss. Aim for a cold evening with the heating on for a few hours beforehand.
  • Avoid sun, rain and strong wind. A wall that has been in the sun stays warm for hours and can hide or imitate heat loss. Wet walls and wind change the surface temperature too.
  • Stand square to the wall where you can, and get the whole feature in the picture (the whole window, not one corner).
  • Shiny surfaces mislead. Glass, polished metal and glazed tiles reflect the sky or your own warmth, so a reading on them is not the surface's real temperature.
  • Use the original files. The temperatures are stored inside the camera's .jpg file. Copy the files from the camera or its memory card. Pictures that have been edited, screenshotted or passed through a messaging app usually lose the temperatures and cannot be processed.

Making a batch

  1. On the home page press Select Images (or drag the pictures onto the box) and choose up to {{ batch_size }} .jpg pictures.
  2. Batch name is optional. A name such as the address makes the batch easy to find later; without one it is listed by its number.
  3. Points to mark automatically sets how readily the site marks a warm place: {% for s in sensitivities %}{{ s.label }}{% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}. Standard suits most surveys, and you can change it afterwards.
  4. Press Process Batch. After a few seconds the batch appears in the list below.

The drive

The drive is shared storage where pictures can wait until someone is ready to make the report. One person can put the pictures there on the night, and anyone in the group can process them later.

Putting pictures on the drive

  1. Type a name in Drive folder, usually the property address. Names you have used before are offered as you type. Leave it empty to use the main folder.
  2. Choose the pictures with Select Images, then press Upload to drive. (With nothing chosen, the button opens a file picker.) There is no limit of {{ batch_size }} here.

Making a batch from the drive

  1. Press Select from drive. Folders are shown as buttons with the number of pictures in each.
  2. Open a folder and tick up to {{ batch_size }} pictures, or press Tick all.
  3. Press Use selected, then Process Batch.

A batch made from a folder takes the folder's name, and the folder name is filled in as the property address. You can change both.

  • Delete selected removes the ticked pictures from the drive for good. Batches already made keep their own copies.
  • {% if delete_after %}Pictures are removed from the drive once a batch has been made from them.{% else %}Pictures stay on the drive after a batch is made{% if keep_days %}, and may be cleared out automatically after about {{ keep_days }} days{% endif %}.{% endif %}
  • If the drive is asleep, the page says Waking the drive and carries on by itself after a minute or two.

Points and notes

Open a batch by clicking its name, or Points & Notes. This page decides what the report says.

About the survey

  • Property address and Inspector / surveyor appear at the top of the report.
  • General survey notes appear on the first page. They start with the date the pictures were taken, when the pictures record one. Add the weather and anything else the reader should know.
  • Each picture also has its own note box for something that applies to that picture only.

The points

Each marked place on a picture has a box beside it. For each one:

  • Type: {{ types|join(', ') }}.
  • Number: number the features of each type from 1. Use the same number when the same feature appears in two pictures, so the report treats it as one window or one door.
  • Priority is worked out from the temperatures. Change it if you know better.
  • Report comment is written for you and can be edited. Reset to the generated text brings the original back.
  • Recommendations: pick from the list for that type, or choose Write my own.
  • Your note: anything else about this point.

The dashed box under each point shows exactly what the report will say about it.

Only points with both a type and a number go into the report. Leave a point without them to keep it out.
  • To add a point the site missed, click that place on the picture. Points you add can be removed with Remove this point.
  • Too many or too few points? Change Points marked automatically and press Re-detect points. Points you added yourself, and all notes, are kept.

Save keeps your work so you can come back to it. Save & Generate Report saves and opens the report.

Temperature scale

  • Automatic for each picture: each picture uses the range that suits it. Good for seeing detail.
  • Same scale on every picture: one range for the whole batch, so a colour means the same temperature on every picture. Good for comparing pictures.
  • Set by hand: type the coldest and warmest temperature. A wider range calms a picture that looks blotchy. A narrower one brings out small differences.

Press Apply colours. Every picture is redrawn, on this page and in the report. Points, notes and temperatures do not change. A picture shown as it came off the camera cannot be re-scaled and has no scale bar.

The report

The report has an introduction and summary, the pictures with their points listed beneath (four pictures to a page), and a closing page of general energy-saving advice.

  • To print or make a PDF, use your browser's print command (Ctrl+P, or Cmd+P on a Mac) and choose your printer or Save as PDF.
  • To change the report, go back to Points & Notes, make the change and press Save & Generate Report again.
  • Open it again at any time with Heat Loss Report on the home page.

Reading the results

A point is a place that is clearly warmer than the wall around it, seen from outside. Its priority comes from how much warmer it is.

The report shows where to look. It is not a building survey or an energy performance certificate. Reflections, recent sunshine, and anything warm near the wall (a flue, an outside light, a parked car) can all produce a warm point that is not heat loss, so use what you saw on the night when deciding what to include.

Your batches

The home page lists every batch{% if groups_on %} belonging to the group shown in the bar at the top{% endif %}, newest first. Everyone{% if groups_on %} in the group{% endif %} sees the same list.

  • Rename gives the batch a name, or changes it.
  • Points & Notes opens the page where the report is prepared.
  • Heat Loss Report opens the finished report, once it has been generated.
  • Batch Data shows the temperature figures for each picture.
  • Delete removes the batch, its pictures and its report for everyone. It cannot be undone, and a link to a deleted batch stops working.

Maximum {{ batch_size }} images per batch Choose fewer pictures, or make two batches.
Batch not found The batch has been deleted{% if groups_on %}, or it belongs to another group (check the bar at the top){% endif %}. Deleted batches cannot be brought back; process the pictures again.
A picture is almost all one colour On the points page set Temperature scale to Set by hand and narrow the range around the temperatures of the wall, then Apply colours.
Too many points, or an obvious one missed Use Re-detect points with Fewer or More, or click the picture to add a point.

The drive is switched off or asleep / did not wake up Wait a few minutes and try again. If it still fails, upload the pictures from your computer instead and tell your organiser.
The drive is not connected to the server, or the site is not allowed to save to it This needs the organiser. Upload from your computer in the meantime.
You are signed in, but not set up yet The email address you signed in with has not been added. Ask your organiser, or log out and sign in with the address they used.
Sign-in required Open the site through its normal web address.
